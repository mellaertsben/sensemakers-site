Request information about the Programme

The transformation needs someone who owns it on your side and isn't selling you anything else. The Programme is that person: a senior lead, one or two days a week, who steers strategy, ownership, adoption and the builders — and hands the role to one of your own people as the operating model learns to carry itself.

  • A monthly rhythm: steering, ownership and operating model, adoption, measurement. A quarterly review with the leadership team, on one page.
  • Vendors and Sprints answer to your lead — including our own builders.
  • Priced on days. A build is never inside the retainer: a Sprint is a separate decision, priced on its own.

You keep: an AI portfolio register with an owner for every use case, an operating model written down as it is decided, playbooks, a value and adoption plan measured monthly, and the quarterly review for the board.

Prove one workflow

AI Transformation Sprint

Six or eight weeks
A team of two or three, plus your product owner
A weekly rate for the window

Request information about the Sprint

One real workflow, redesigned and running with agents in your environment, measured against its own baseline. A Sprint is a window with a question, not a deliverable with a price: we frame the question in week one, build in weekly increments with your users in the room, measure, and decide together — scale, stop, or iterate.

  • Week 1: the question, the success criteria, the baseline measured, the guardrails.
  • Weeks 2–5: build with the users; a working demo every Friday.
  • Week 6: the redesigned workflow runs next to the old one. Baseline against after, plainly.
  • Weeks 7–8: harden, hand over, decide.

You keep: the redesigned blueprint, the working proof of concept on your accounts, the agent spec and register entry, the measurement, a run and ownership plan, and a decision memo. Code and data are yours from day one.

What we don't promise: a production system, or a fixed deliverable for a fixed sum. If you need a fixed price, we'll say so and bring a partner who sells one.
