Privacy

What sensemakers.be collects about you, why, and what you can ask of us. The short version: very little, and no cookies.

Who we are

Sensemakers decides how your data is used. Questions go to hello@sensemakers.be.

When you email us

We keep your name, email address and what you write, to answer you and follow up. We keep it while we are in touch, and at most two years after the last contact. Our email is hosted by Combell.

When you book a call

Booking runs on Calendly, which receives your name, email address and the time you pick, under its own privacy policy. We use it only to hold the call.

When you visit the site

We count visits with Plausible, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data. We don't count or track visits. Our host, GitHub Pages, logs visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. The fonts load from our own site.

The readiness check

Your answers stay in your browser. We don't receive or store them.

Cookies

This site sets none, so there is no cookie banner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to hello@sensemakers.be. We answer within a month. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority, dataprotectionauthority.be.
