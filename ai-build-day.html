AI Build Day

A hackathon on your own problems. Teams build working prototypes without code, and every prototype leaves with an owner and a decision date.

  • Challenges, access and guardrails prepared weeks ahead
  • A short builder course, then building with us as coaches
  • A pitch to your leadership, and a decision within 30 days
