Who it's for

For leadership teams that want a direction, not a demo.

The organisation

Roughly 200 to 2,000 people.

Big enough for AI to be arriving in several teams at once; small enough that one leadership team can still decide. A sponsor at C-level, or one below, who owns the outcome.

What it sounds like

"We're not sure how far to take AI, or where to start."

Also: "We don't know which agents to build." "Every vendor has a pitch and we can't tell what's real." "Everyone is talking about it and nobody owns it."

Not for

A build quote.

The Scan decides what to prove and who owns it. Building is not what we do: when it's needed, we bring a partner and steer them from your side. And it needs a sponsor who can decide; without one, we'll say so before we start.

What happens

Three weeks, one rhythm.

About twelve hours of your leadership team's time in total, and one sponsor who owns the outcome. We do the rest.

Before we start

Intake

An intake form, access to your tool inventory, the interviewee list, and the readiness check filled in by your leadership team.

Week 1

Ambition and baseline

Six to eight leadership interviews. A snapshot of AI tooling and spend. A workflow inventory with two or three team leads. Readiness scored.

Week 2

Opportunity map

Ten to fifteen workflows scored on value, feasibility and ownership. Agent fit assessed. The first use case to prove identified, and who should own it. Capability gaps named.

Week 3

Decision

The 90-day plan with owners. A two-hour playback with the leadership team. The decision memo: what to do alone, what to do with help, what to leave alone.

In plain terms

What we promise. What we don't.

We promise

A decision-ready plan in three weeks.

And an honest answer if the answer is "not yet". A Scan that ends in "leave it alone for a year" is a good Scan.

We don't promise

A build quote or a technology choice.

We'll name the first workflow to prove and who should own it. What it costs to build comes from the partner who builds it, not from us.

What comes next: the plan is executable alone. Most teams continue with the Programme, a lead on their side of the table, or prove the first use case with their own people in an AI Build Day.
