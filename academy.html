Five formats.

Every course runs on real tasks from your organisation.

For everyone
Two half-days

AI Foundations

The basics every employee needs: how AI works, where it fails, what never goes in, and how to use it in your own tool. Supports the AI literacy the EU AI Act asks of employers.

Read more

Half one, Understand: how the models work, where they fail, data and confidentiality, and your own usage policy. Half two, Use: your own tasks in your own tool, from mail and meetings to reusable assistants. Each half can be booked on its own; together they make the full day we recommend. Every participant gets an attestation, and you get a record of your literacy measures.

One day

Thinking with AI

Better decisions with AI, not faster mistakes: a simple decision process, the new ways AI can mislead you, and practice on your own decisions.

Read more

AI moves thinking upstream, into the question, the framing and the evidence. We work through a six-step decision process with the tools for each step, and the traps of working with AI: agreeing too easily, trusting the machine, handing over the thinking. You leave with a personal decision scorecard and a checklist for your own work.

Both also run as a closed session for one leadership team.

For teams
Half a day to a day

AI for your role

One team, its real work: where AI helps, where it doesn't, and who checks what. Tracks for sales, marketing and software delivery.

Read more

We map the team's workflows, then practise in the team's own tool on tasks brought that morning, with the risks that belong to the role: customer data, compliance, tone. The team leaves with a role playbook and a set of assistants per workflow. Software teams work in Codex, Cursor or Claude Code.

One day

AI Build Day From 2027

A hackathon on your own problems: teams build working prototypes without code, and every prototype leaves with an owner and a decision date.

Read more

Twenty to forty people in teams of four or five, after a few weeks of preparing the challenges, the access and the guardrails. The day opens with a short builder course, then it's building, with us as coaches, and a pitch to your leadership. Every prototype gets an owner and a decision within 30 days: continue, hand over or stop.

For the organisation
Two days, then six months

AI Champions Programme From 2027

We train a small group of your people to run the basics themselves, then meet them monthly for half a year.

Read more

Six to twelve champions learn to give AI Foundations, coach colleagues and collect use cases for leadership. They get the material under licence, so it keeps running without us, plus a monthly session and a shared library of cases.
