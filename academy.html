Formats

Six ways to build it.

From ninety minutes to a company-wide programme, and one that keeps going. All of them work on real tasks from your organisation. Prices on request.

Lead
For leadership teams

Leadership cohort

Two half-days plus toolkit
Up to 16 leaders
Your leadership team, or an open cohort with peers from other companies

How AI changes decisions, ownership and the operating model, and what that asks of the people at the top. Leaders work on their own area: where AI already is, who owns it, how far to take it, what to deliberately leave alone.

  • Half-day one: the landscape, the ownership shift, and a readiness read of your own organisation.
  • Half-day two: judging AI-shaped inputs before you act on them, and the ambition and decision rights for your area.

You leave with: an ambition statement for your area, a readiness read, and the habits to judge AI output before you act on it.

For a leadership team or an event

Executive briefing

60–90 minutes
Keynote or closed-door briefing
Tailored to your sector

What is real and what is a wrapper, how the ownership of AI is shifting inside organisations, where the value is showing up and where it isn't, and where to start. A straight read, with a stance.

You leave with: a shared picture across the leadership team, and three questions to take back to your own organisation.

Work
For anyone, any role

Hands-on session

Half a day, on site or online
Up to 12 people, mixed roles welcome
On the tools you already have

Personal productivity with AI, on your own work. Writing, analysis, research, meetings, the admin that eats the week: participants bring real tasks and leave with them done differently. Which tool for what, how to brief it, how hard to check the result, and where to stop.

  • Every exercise runs on a task the participant brought that morning.
  • A personal set of routines to keep, not a list of prompts to forget.

You leave with: three or four tasks you now do with AI, a check for each, and the confidence to find the next one yourself.

For one team

Team workshop

One day, on site
One team, up to 14 people
Built on their real work

One team, its actual workflow. Experience first, theory second: participants work with AI on their own material, red-team each other's output, then decide together where AI sits in the work, who owns what, and where the checkpoint goes.

  • Exercises drawn from the team's live workflow, not from a slide deck.
  • Where the checkpoint sits for every task, and how hard to check.

You leave with: role playbooks and output checks the team keeps, and a shared view of who owns what.

For a department or the whole company

Workforce programme

Blended, 6–10 weeks
Short live sessions, practice on real tasks
Champions in every team

Skill-building at scale, designed to run without a consultant in every room. Short live sessions per role, practice on the participants' own tasks between them, champions trained to keep it going, and adoption measured against a baseline rather than counted in attendance.

  • Role-specific tracks: what a finance analyst needs is not what a sales rep needs.
  • An adoption funnel with a real denominator: aware, tried, active, embedded.

You leave with: a workforce that changed how it works, champions who own the next wave, and the numbers to show it.

Keep up
For the organisation, over time

Frontier watch

By subscription, monthly
Written for leaders, not engineers
With a line to us for the questions it raises

Most of what changes in AI each month doesn't matter to you. Some of it matters a lot, and it rarely announces itself. Frontier watch is a regular read on what changed, what it means for an organisation like yours, and what, if anything, to do about it: adopt, wait, or switch.

  • What moved: models, tools, pricing, regulation, and what is a wrapper.
  • What it means for your roadmap, your tools and your people, with a recommendation.
  • A standing line to us: send the question the briefing raised, get a straight answer.

You keep: a roadmap that stays current, decisions revisited on purpose rather than by surprise, and a leadership team that hears about the next shift before it hits.

How it's different

Your work, not a demo.

Experience before theory

Do it, then name it.

Participants work with AI on their own tasks first, and get the framework afterwards. The lesson lands because they just lived it.

Real work, not demos

Your files, your workflows.

No generic exercises. Every session runs on material from the participants' actual jobs, so what they learn on Tuesday they use on Wednesday.

Measured

Adoption, not attendance.

We baseline before and measure after: who is using what, how often, and whether it is embedded in the work. Attendance is not the metric.
