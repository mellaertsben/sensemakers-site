About us

AI is ready. We help your organisation catch up.

Sensemakers keeps leadership teams up to date with AI, teaches people to work with it, and helps run the change. We're practitioners who still do the work we teach.

Who you'll work with.

BM

Ben Mellaerts

Strategy, adoption and change

Ben started as a data analyst at Telenet and moved into business analysis and AI adoption. At Mediahuis he drives the adoption of in-house generative AI tools among some 2,000 journalists across more than 30 brands, with hands-on sessions in five countries. He also hosts the DataTopics Unplugged podcast.

How we work.

Hiking the Grand Canyon beats looking at a photo of it. We teach AI by doing it, on your own work, in plain words.

Still doing the work.

None of us teaches full-time. We all keep working on real AI projects, so what we teach comes from this month, not last year.

Responsible by default.
