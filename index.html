Why now

The technology is no longer the bottleneck. The organisation is.

Generative AI put building and using AI within reach of every function. The tools arrived faster than the ownership, the skills and the habits to use them well. That gap is where the value gets lost. It is also the gap you can close fastest: not with another tool, but with people who can lead, work and judge with AI.
