Why now

Every organisation is fighting the same four problems.

Generative AI put building and using AI within reach of every function. That is good news, and it is exactly why the organisation, not the technology, is now the bottleneck.

Shadow AI

Teams stand up tools and agents with their own credentials. Useful, invisible, and unowned, until something goes wrong.

A landscape that moves monthly

Models, tools and vendors change faster than any roadmap. Choosing means re-choosing, and most roadmaps aren't built for that.

The jungle

Dozens of subscriptions, overlapping tools, half-built agents. Nobody can compare them, so nobody can decide what to keep.

Access without adoption

Everyone has a licence. A handful of people actually changed how they work, and the value stays with them.

How we work

Built to compound.

Three principles behind every engagement, whether we are looking at one team or the whole organisation.

Compounding, by design

Every step makes the next one cheaper.

Each engagement leaves templates, owners, metrics and skills behind. The second team moves faster than the first; the fifth barely needs help. Transformation that gets easier as it goes, not harder.

Antifragile, not just robust

Stronger with every change.

We design for the next model release and the next surprise: decisions that can be revisited, agents that can be switched off, people who can judge. An organisation that gains from change rather than breaking under it.

Done with you, owned by you

Your people, in the room.

We work with your team, not around it, and transfer ownership as we go. No dependency, no lock-in, and a baseline on day one so the result is measured, not claimed.
