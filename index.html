The problem

AI tools are already in the business. The organisation hasn't caught up.

Your people use AI every day. Strategy, ownership, skills and ways of working haven't moved at the same pace, so the gains stay small, the costs creep, and nobody is quite sure who decides what. We guide the transformation end to end, and stay until it holds.

Why now

Every organisation is fighting the same four problems.

Generative AI put building and using AI within reach of every function. That is good news, and it is exactly why the organisation, not the technology, is now the bottleneck.

Shadow AI

Teams stand up tools and agents with their own credentials. Useful, invisible, and unowned, until something goes wrong.

A landscape that moves monthly

Models, tools and vendors change faster than any roadmap. Choosing means re-choosing, and most roadmaps aren't built for that.

The jungle

Dozens of subscriptions, overlapping tools, half-built agents. Nobody can compare them, so nobody can decide what to keep.

Access without adoption

Everyone has a licence. A handful of people actually changed how they work, and the value stays with them.

How we work

Four moves, measured throughout.

The same four moves whether we are looking at one team or the whole organisation. A baseline on day one, a number you agreed up front at the end.

1 · Diagnose

Where you stand

Strategy, ownership, adoption, skills and spend, read honestly and measured. What is working, what is shadow, what is waste.

2 · Decide

Ambition and priorities

How far you want to take AI, what you deliberately won't automate, and what comes first. A roadmap and the decision rights to run it.

3 · Deliver

One team at a time

Sprints on real work: agents placed, owners named, people enabled, results measured against the baseline. Built with your team.

4 · Embed

Make it hold

Ownership, rituals, skills and a register that outlive the project, so the change keeps working without us in the room.

Compounding, by design

Every step makes the next one cheaper.

Each sprint leaves templates, owners, metrics and skills behind. The second team moves faster than the first; the fifth barely needs us. Transformation that gets easier as it goes, not harder.

Antifragile, not just robust

Stronger with every change.

We design for the next model release and the next surprise: decisions that can be revisited, agents that can be switched off, people who can judge. An organisation that gains from change rather than breaking under it.
