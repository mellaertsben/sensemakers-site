Questions.

Do we need an IT or data team?

No. We start from proven tools and the set-up you already have.

Can we do only the roadmap?

Yes. Phase one stands on its own. Many start there and decide afterwards.

What if the roadmap says “don't build yet”?

Then we say so, and tell you what to fix first.

Who builds the application?

An implementation partner we know and trust. We stay your point of contact throughout.

Can our people learn it too?

Yes. AI Foundations fits well at the start of phase two.

And after six months?

You run it yourselves. Frontier Watch keeps your leadership up to date.
