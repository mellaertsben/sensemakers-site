Request information about the Programme

The transformation needs someone who owns it on your side and isn't selling you anything else. The Programme is that person: a senior lead, one or two days a week, who runs the plan that came out of your Leadership Sprint or Scan, activates the Academy formats as the plan needs them, and hands the role to one of your own people as the organisation learns to carry it.

  • The use case portfolio: discovery, prioritisation, an owner per case, and the quarterly page for the board.
  • Tooling and spend: the Audit, the roadmap, what you own and what you rent.
  • Adoption: Foundations for everyone through champions, role tracks per team, an AI Build Day on the shortlist, Thinking with AI for the people who decide.
  • Leading AI in your team: a module for team leads. Norms, one redesigned workflow, what to delegate to AI and what to people.
  • Light governance: a usage policy people can name, the literacy duty, what never goes in.
  • Measurement: adoption with a real denominator, value against a baseline.
  • Build partners, steered from your side, when something needs building.

You keep: a portfolio register with an owner for every use case, the plan as it is decided, playbooks, an adoption and value plan measured monthly, and the quarterly review for the board.

Priced on days. A build is never inside the retainer: if something needs building, we bring a partner and it is a separate decision, with your lead on your side of it.
