Think

Better decisions, fewer costly mistakes.

AI moves thinking upstream, into the question, the framing and the evidence. Half a day on thinking before you prompt and checking after it answers.

  • Before you prompt: frame the question yourself
  • After it answers: spot the new traps
  • Practice on decisions from your own work

After this module, participants can

1Frame a question so AI helps rather than steers
2Use a simple decision process, with the right check at each step
3Recognize the traps: agreeing too easily, trusting the machine, handing over the thinking
4Check their own decisions with a personal checklist
