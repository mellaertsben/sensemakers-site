About us

The technology is here. Your people make the difference.

Technology alone delivers little. Your people are the lever: make them stronger, and you get more out of every technology, step by step. That is why Sensemakers teaches leaders and their teams to think and work with AI, on their own work and in the tools they already use. We're practitioners who still do the work we teach.

Our vision

Organizations where people lead AI, not the other way around.

Our mission

We equip leaders and teams with the skills and judgment to get the most out of AI in a responsible way.
