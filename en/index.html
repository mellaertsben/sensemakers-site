Questions

Doubt is good. We even teach it.

Your question isn't here? Ask it on the free call.

We already did an AI training.

A good start. But those trainings often stay theoretical, and a few weeks later the fundamentals are gone. We start from digital, systems thinking and statistics, with exercises and memory aids. So we skip no steps, and it sticks.

We don't have Copilot yet, or any tool at all.

Understand and Think work with any tool, or none. We run Build in the tool your team uses.

Our people aren't technical.

They don't need to be. Only Claude Code is for technical teams. Everything else is for everyone.

Can we start with one module?

Yes. Every module stands on its own. After Build you can add a follow-up session, a few weeks later.

How many people per session?

Anything is possible. We prefer groups of up to 20 people per session, so everyone gets the most out of it. For larger groups we plan several sessions.

What does it cost?

From per half-day module, for one group. It depends on the modules and the number of groups. The proposal has one fixed price.

Does this count for the AI Act?

Yes. Article 4 asks employers to take measures that support the AI literacy of their people. Understand is such a measure, and you get a record for your files.

Which language?

Dutch or English. French on request.
